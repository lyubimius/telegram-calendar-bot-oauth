Telegram Calendar Bot

A private Telegram assistant that helps its owner view and manage Google Calendar events and Google Tasks.

What the app does

  • Shows calendar events for today and tomorrow.
  • Creates, reschedules, and deletes events after confirmation.
  • Creates and completes Google Tasks.
  • Sends a daily summary and notifications about newly detected events.

Personal use

This application is not offered as a public service. It is used by a small set of known accounts approved by the owner.

Google data

The app requests only the Google Calendar and Google Tasks permissions required for its features. Google data is used solely to perform commands initiated by the approved user and to generate requested notifications.

Privacy
